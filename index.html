03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I like the messy
part at the start.

The competing perspectives. The complicated workflows. The question behind the brief.

I’m a human-centred designer based in Perth, with experience across mining, fintech, sports and government. I combine research and facilitation with hands-on UX and UI design to help teams move from uncertainty to practical improvements.

At Fortescue, I lead discovery and ways-of-working workshops, map frontline workflows and bring designers together through the Design Chapter. Previously, at Racing WA, I combined journey mapping and service blueprinting with design leadership across a complex customer experience.

I care about making room for people’s perspectives and helping teams build a shared way forward.
