Ailo / Workflow design · Research · Product design

Making complex financial workflows easier to use.

At Ailo, I led product experience across trust accounting, reconciliation, reporting, compliance and payments. My work connected research, workflow mapping, prioritisation and detailed interaction design.

01 / The challenge

Financial tasks with too much friction.

Property managers were navigating complex financial processes, while renters needed a clearer way to complete payments. The experience needed to reduce errors and repetitive work without losing the detail these tasks required.

02 / Research & mapping

Find the patterns behind the problems.

I reviewed more than 100 feedback tickets, interviewed and observed users, and mapped workflows to understand where tasks became difficult.

The research highlighted four areas: trust accounting, GST tracking, rent payments and reporting. I used those findings to focus design and validation on high-impact customer problems.

03 / Prioritise & co-design

Turn the evidence into a roadmap.

I owned the domain design roadmap and worked with product managers and engineering to sequence customer problems and validation work.

Collaborative workshops, journey mapping, sketches and wireframes helped the team explore alternatives early and align on what to test.

04 / Design & test

Make the complicated parts clearer.

The work included clearer GST status and onboarding, simpler payment flows, and report layouts that made filters and information easier to understand.

I used prototypes and usability testing to challenge assumptions before development, including Maze for unmoderated studies and Askable for participant recruitment. I applied the internal design system and worked with developers through specification, implementation and QA.

05 / Outcomes & learning

Measure what changed after release.

Shipped improvements contributed to 25% fewer accounting errors, 20% higher rent-payment completion and up to 50% improved reporting efficiency.

Post-release measurement helped us understand task success and customer behaviour and informed subsequent improvements. These outcomes reinforced the value of treating workflow design as an ongoing process of learning and iteration.
