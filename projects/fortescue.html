01 / The context

Complex systems. People doing critical work.

I work in autonomous mining technology and fleet management. The people using these systems include mine controllers, pit technicians and operational subject-matter experts.

The work includes understanding frontline tasks and translating those needs into clear workflows and product experiences. Project details and screens are confidential; this case study describes my approach at a high level.

02 / Understand & map

Start close to the work.

Through site observation and discovery with frontline users and operational specialists, I map critical workflows and identify pain points.

This work has helped identify more than 100 UX/UI and workflow issues. I translate user evidence into practical recommendations, prioritised by user impact and operational risk.

03 / Co-design ways of working

Build the process with the team.

Following an organisational restructure, I have led collaborative process workshops with my direct team to explore what is working, what is not and what could improve.

Rather than prescribing a process, I create space for the team to share perspectives and agree how we should work together to achieve our outcomes. We revisit and refine those practices iteratively.

I also lead weekly discovery workshops and facilitate sessions with leadership and broader stakeholders to understand needs and inform design decisions.

04 / Connect to delivery

Make the insights usable.

For the Pit Technician application, I defined core workflows, product structure and interaction patterns. I partner with product, engineering and validation to translate design into requirements and acceptance criteria.

Working through these connections helps user needs remain visible as the work moves into development.

05 / Strengthen practice

Help other teams use the methods.

I lead the Fortescue Design Chapter, bringing designers together weekly to strengthen shared practice. This includes developing discovery templates and toolkits for teams without a designer.

I also contribute to the design system to support reuse and more consistent delivery.

The work is ongoing. The emphasis is on learning from the people doing the work, building shared ownership of improvements and connecting design practice to team outcomes.
