My Foodie Box / Research · Mobile UX · UI design

Taking a familiar service into a mobile experience.

I led the design of a cross-platform mobile experience for My Foodie Box, translating an established web service into an app designed around the tasks customers needed on a smaller screen.

ROLE

Senior Product Designer

WHEN

Six-week design sprint

FOCUS

Research · Mobile UX · UI design

IN THIS CASE STUDY

UnderstandShape & designCollaborate & deliver

01 / Understand

Preserve familiarity. Improve the experience.

I audited the existing website, navigation and core flows, identifying meal browsing, order customisation and subscription management as important tasks.

With limited time for primary research, I combined competitor analysis, market research and user reviews to inform a lean discovery approach.

02 / Shape & design

Design for the small screen.

The principles were clarity, transparency and personalisation. I focused on straightforward navigation and clear information about pricing, meal availability and subscription options.

I designed onboarding, meal selection, filtering, customisation and checkout flows. Reusable Figma components supported consistency and rapid iteration.

03 / Collaborate & deliver

Create a shared view of the solution.

I partnered with developers, business analysts and testers, sharing interactive prototypes with stakeholders to gather feedback and align the team.

The six-week sprint delivered the MVP app design and a reusable component library. Early feedback highlighted navigation and transparency, while further iteration was planned around post-launch learning.
